FORMULATION SCIENCE

Why pH 5.5 Matters for Your Body

Healthy human skin maintains a slightly acidic surface environment with a natural pH of approximately 5.5. Traditional harsh soaps disrupt this protective mantle, leading to dryness, irritation, and breakouts.

Formulated in Maryland, USA, each Avien 5.5 product respects your skin barrier while delivering concentrated active ingredients to target hyperpigmentation, rough texture, body breakouts, and loss of elasticity.
